*** CORRECTION, 3 AUGUST 2026: THE CENTRAL RESULT OF THIS PAPER IS
WITHDRAWN. ***

The reported finding — that division regularity (Clock) preceded
spatial organization (Map) in 1,845 of 1,845 runs, binomial
p = 8.01e-146 — was produced by measurement artifacts in my own code,
not by the dynamics it was attributed to. Eight defects are documented
in the correction notice now prepended to the PDF, each sufficient on
its own to produce the reported figure. The principal one: the phase
detector latched the second predicate only if the first had already
latched, so 100% was the only outcome the measurement could return.

Corrected figure: measured without that gate, the ordering holds in
approximately 1% of runs. The reverse ordering is also unestablished —
this model cannot settle the question either way, and a follow-up
experiment indicates the question is malformed as posed: the spatial
statistic becomes measurable at ~150 ticks, the division-regularity
statistic at ~4,450, and the only replacement metric that could be
validated at ~24,000. Comparing which crosses its threshold first
measures that ratio of timescales, not the dynamics.

Two cross-validations this paper relied on can no longer be checked: no
source code survives for the companion Vesicle Division or Engine
studies, so neither the CV = 0.06 agreement cited in Section 4.1 nor
the g = 4.07 comparison in the Table 1 caption can be verified.

The paper text below is left unaltered and is retained deliberately:
its data and code are the evidence for the correction. Found by
re-analysis of my own published code and data; the repository was
public throughout and the decisive evidence was in the paper's own data
files from the day of posting.

Full audit and all raw outputs: https://github.com/AVADSA25/genesis-engine
(directory paper/v6_supplement/). A companion methods paper, "The
Detector Was the Result: Eight Measurement Artifacts, and a Question
That Cannot Be Asked This Way", documents the audit in full, including
the errors made while performing it.
